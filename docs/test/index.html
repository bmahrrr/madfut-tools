How results work

Every result assumes you both play perfectly. Each lead and reply is checked, including holding cards back.

  • Win: you win whatever they do, as long as you follow the moves here.
  • Draw: perfect play from both sides ends level.
  • Loss: they win if they play perfectly. A mistake from them can still hand you the game.

It only holds against that exact team. A different team, or a mistake from you, can change it.
